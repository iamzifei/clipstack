⌘1What it is

One clipboard slot is not enough.

Copy a link, then an email address, and the link is gone. ClipStack is a small menu-bar app that saves every copy as it happens — so nothing you copied is lost, and any of it is one shortcut away.

⌘3How to use it

Three keys. That's it.

Download, drag ClipStack to Applications, open it. It lives in the menu bar — no Dock icon, no window to manage.

⌘C

1 · Copy, as always

Nothing to learn. Every copy is saved quietly in the background.

⇧⌘V

2 · See everything

The panel opens over your app. ↑ ↓ or type to search; the right side previews it all.

↩⌘V

3 · Pick and paste

↩ puts it back on the clipboard, formatting intact. Or let ↩ paste for you (Settings).

⌘4Keyboard only

Hold ⇧⌘. Tap V. Let go.

Like ⌘Tab, for your clipboard: keep ⇧⌘ held and tap V to step back through history, release to paste. A single press still just opens the panel.

⌘5Beyond the basics

Small moves that add up.

Change it before you paste

⌘K

Case, trim, join lines, JSON, URL-encode, colour formats, text from a screenshot (on-device OCR).

Every entry is tagged

⇥

URL, EMAIL, CODE, COLOR, TEXT, IMAGE, FILE. Tab shows one type at a time.

Merge several into one

⌘M

Mark entries, press ↩ — copied as one, in the order you marked them.

Float it next to your work

⌘O

Any entry becomes a small always-on-top note. ⌘T keeps the whole panel on top.
